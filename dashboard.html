Metric definitions

Collection rate: capped payments against installments due by the cutoff ÷ scheduled installments due by the cutoff. Overdue: unpaid scheduled amounts strictly before the cutoff; installments due today have DPD 0. Outstanding: all scheduled unpaid installments, including future installments. 30+ share: outstanding scheduled installment balance of loans with DPD ≥ 30 ÷ all outstanding scheduled installment balance.

Split payments are aggregated before joining. Future payments are excluded. Overpayments are flagged separately. Reconciliation: scheduled total = outstanding + observed payments − excess. Money is stored as integer paise. These definitions are demo contracts, not lender or regulatory accounting definitions.
